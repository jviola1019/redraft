# Field-goal pilot protocol

Freeze this protocol before reading held-out outcomes. Use nflverse NFL regular-season play-by-play from 2001 through 2017. Fit from earlier seasons only, with no within-season updates. The first prospective baseline is 2005, so 2005–2013 is the initial eligible kicker history and 2014–2017 is the fixed evaluation window. Each year forecasts use prior completed seasons, including previous evaluation years in the original order.

Outcome is make vs miss or block among recorded field-goal attempts. Exclude plays outside the regular season, duplicates by game and play ID, unrecognized outcome, missing or implausible distance (outside 17–75 yards), and missing kicker ID. Report exclusion counts. Season and play keys prevent history from being drawn from the same year. Extra points are outside scope.

Baseline is a logistic distance curve with terms for distance in tens of yards centered at 40 and positive hinges at 35, 45 and 55 yards. Fit on the prior four seasons with scikit-learn LogisticRegression (C=10, lbfgs). A rolling window allows era drift. Candidate adds a kicker intercept on the logit scale, fitted as a penalized offset to past predictions from those seasons' own prospective baseline curves. Use a zero-centered normal penalty with standard deviation 0.45, weight prior attempts by 0.8^(age−1), and assign zero to kickers with no prior attempts. These settings are fixed before evaluation.

Compare paired mean log loss (primary) and Brier score (secondary), candidate minus baseline. Report yearly results, distance-bucket counts and outcome/prediction rates, and counts for previously seen vs unseen kickers. Estimate primary uncertainty via a percentile interval from 5,000 paired bootstrap samples of entire kicker clusters (seed 2018). The descriptive interval conditions on these seasons and does not account for shared season shocks. Advance the research candidate only if the primary interval is entirely below zero and the candidate improves at least three of four individual seasons. Report all outcomes regardless. Even a pass validates prediction of selected NFL attempts, not prospect ability or a game/runtime adjustment.
