# Chronological scoring from drive segments

Protocol fixed before fitting or scoring forecasts. Compare one source-backed drive-segment candidate with the established chronological rounded-normal comparator from `analytics/score_marginal.py`. The prior marginal recalibration failed late-window shutout Brier. The prior whole-game donor failed spread and side accuracy and materially suppressed team-season dispersion. This experiment adds drive outcomes, a joint draw of game drive counts, and the comparator's shared team-season margin shocks. Use the existing 2013–2015 and 2016–2017 assessment windows and 4,096 draws. These are previously examined seasons.

Use nflverse archived regular-season play-by-play for 2001–2017 and the existing 1999–2017 games table for history and final outcomes. Fit each origin exclusively on earlier seasons. The candidate fits an unconditional joint pace distribution and separate home- and away-possession categorical tables from its three preceding seasons. Each regulation fixed-drive segment is represented by points credited to its possession team and opponent. The possession team is the first listed on a non-kickoff snap, punt, or field goal in the segment, falling back to the first listed possession team. Miscellaneous segments with no team and no points are excluded. A points-bearing unassigned segment, nonmonotone boundary scoring, missing game, or disagreement with the existing final score invalidates that game for candidate fitting. Report losses by season and abort if fewer than 95% of games are valid in any training season. Overtime plays enter the reconciliation only. No test drives supply forecasts.

Sample historical paired regulation drive counts jointly. Fit separate empirical categorical credit laws by side. Exponentially tilt each side's category probabilities on own points, matching the side's baseline expected score adjusted for the mean opposite-possession points and divided by the mean count. Allow the baseline's shared signed team-season shock to move those means by half its differential. Clip target points per drive to a small interior buffer (0.05 points) of the observed side support at the extremes. Sample drive categories independently conditional on possession side, sampled counts, and shocks. Apply the comparator's same tie resolution policy and common random tie draws. A regulation drive segment is a grouping defined by the data producer. This coarse model has no play sequencing, clock, or opponent response to game state.

Adoption screen per window requires lower joint energy score and combined-score CRPS, shutout Brier no worse, margin/home/away CRPS and all MAEs within 1%, 90% coverage error within one percentage point of baseline, and both team-season win and point SD within 5% of baseline with positive same-team residual correlation. Report normalized ratios, rates, and observed calibration context where available. Energy score uses both home/away totals jointly and one independent within-fixture pairing from the two halves of the draw stream for its expectation estimate. A screen pass would justify fresh validation only. Freeze seed, windows, and all decision criteria; no candidate tuning after seeing assessment scores.

Sources: [nflfastR drive corrections](https://github.com/nflverse/nflfastR/blob/master/R/helper_add_fixed_drives.R), [nflverse archived play-by-play](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases/tag/pbp), [Gneiting and Raftery, 2007](https://sites.stat.washington.edu/raftery/Research/PDF/Gneiting2007jasa.pdf).
