Freeze this protocol before generating distributions or examining their scores. This is a retrospective comparison on previously studied cohorts.

Baseline uses the exact frozen 2013 production QB donors, the 200-snap rookie-role split and production exposure policy. Use the same production-era cohort identity rules for additional donors and preserve the baseline rows verbatim. The mature donor years end by 2020 in this study, so all are observed under 16-game schedules. Use position-wide donors if a stratum is empty. Do not tune a weight or a threshold.

A secondary draft-time sensitivity assumes the current schedule length persists over the horizon (16 games in 2019 and 2020, 17 in 2021). Use unchanged recorded actuals. Reconcile season-level game coverage through 2025 before assigning zero to a known player absent from a season.
