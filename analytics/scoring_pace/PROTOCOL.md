# Remaining regulation possessions and score state

Model, windows, metrics, and thresholds were frozen before assessment. The exact pre-play score specification below was fixed before rerunning the assessment, after an alignment audit found occasional discrepancies in the completed-segment approximation. Use the checked-in clocked-drive extraction, nflverse regular-season play-by-play archives for 2010–2017, and the existing schedule and final-score table for reconciliation. Require at least 95% of scheduled games to reconcile in each season, counting clock failures as exclusions. Use the preceding three seasons at each 2013–2017 origin, with 2013–2015 and 2016–2017 assessment windows. These years were previously examined for score forecasts. This test evaluates a separate target.

At each eligible drive boundary, predict the number of later retained regulation fixed-drive segments in that game. Exclude the current segment from the target. Treat each retained segment as one possession opportunity under the producer's corrected-drive convention. The prediction uses the first eligible possession play's clock, and the relative cumulative score recorded immediately before that play. The score is carried forward from the preceding play rows and shifted by one row before selecting anchors, so it cannot include a score on the anchor play or any later play. Exclude overtime from opportunities and labels. Include end-of-half and kneel-only retained segments under the stock extraction. Use game rows only after reconciliation against the final score. No final score or eventual drive count is supplied as a predictor.

Compare a clock-only least-squares model with a clock-and-score model. Use piecewise linear clock basis functions with knots every five minutes from zero to 60. For the candidate add the same functions interacted with a deficit of at least eight or a lead of at least eight from the current possession side's perspective. The reference group is within seven points. Give each game total weight one and divide its weight equally across eligible drives. Apply ridge penalties equivalent to two units of game weight per score-interaction coefficient and 0.001 per clock coefficient, with the same clock penalty in both models. Bound forecasts below at zero. No tuning on the origins.

Score by mean squared error and mean absolute error, first averaging across drives within each game and then across games. Also report RMSE, held-out clock-band summaries and per-origin results. Quantify score-state changes as fitted differences against the within-seven reference at five, ten, and 30 minutes remaining by origin. For each assessment window, compute paired candidate-minus-baseline 95% percentile intervals for MSE and MAE from 2,000 draws resampling whole scheduled weeks within each season. This retains dependencies among drives in the same game and games in the same week. Dependence across weeks and generalization across seasons are not captured. Evidence of reproducible forecast gain requires both MSE intervals below zero and neither MAE more than 1% worse. This result does not validate a simulated strategy, a duration model, or runtime use.
