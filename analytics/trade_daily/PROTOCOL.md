# Frozen Daily trade policy protocol

Use all six saved November 1–6, 2026 local Daily fixtures from the multi-day rubric audit, with their stored team, year, seed, blind salt and version pins. Freeze policy rules before running feasibility or simulations. Choices use publicly visible ownership and pick order, the game's offer evaluation, and the same publicly disclosed scouted probability board as the rubric's Scouted probability policy. There are no player identity or outcome inputs to trades, and all three arms have the same board and scouting allocation per date.

- **No trade.** Complete with the board and normal CPU advance without proposing a user trade.
- **Trade up.** At the opening state, attempt to exchange the user's earliest pick plus one or two later picks for one earlier pick. Require a move of 1–32 pick positions inclusive and a give-to-receive chart value ratio at most 1.35. Choose the largest move, then lowest ratio, then ascending pick-order tuple. If the engine accepts none, follow no trade.
- **Trade down.** Advance to the first user selection. Exchange that selection for two or three later picks from the same partner. The first incoming pick must be 1–32 positions later and the give-to-receive ratio must be at most 1.35. Choose the smallest move down, then highest total incoming chart value, then ascending pick-order tuple. If the engine accepts none, follow no trade.

Enumerate the structural candidates, call runtime proposal evaluation on each, and count structural candidates, candidates within the ratio cap, engine approvals and declines within the cap. Record reasons and a representative rejected offer when possible. Reject invalid proposals or conservation failures as audit errors. If an offer qualifies, execute it with the runtime application function and confirm the pick IDs, rounds, completed selections and historical baseline remain unchanged in that transaction, ownership changes match exactly the offer, and the ledger reconciles. Finish the draft with official auto-completion and keep CPU trades enabled throughout. Each strategy can execute at most one user trade.

Score all 256 official paired replicas at the published rubric weights. Report policy availability before interpreting score or wins. Compare each strategy to no trade using the integer decision score, its five weighted components, and the mean difference between the policies' alternate five-year win totals on identical replica indices. Retain direct paired path deltas, their empirical 10th–90th percentile range, simulation standard error, and first/second 128-draw checks. Report frames both with no-trade fallbacks included and conditional on execution. Counts of higher, tied and lower results are descriptive for these fixed dates. No population inference, causal real-world claims or product recommendation is determined by this audit.
