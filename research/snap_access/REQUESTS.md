# Draft data access requests

These drafts are ready for a submitter to paste into the listed channel. Supply the name, contact details and any affiliation requested by the form. Each draft asks for information and a quote, without committing to any fee or terms.

## Teamworks enterprise football — first request for both seasons

[Teamworks contact form](https://teamworks.com/contact) · [Teamworks NFL player personnel](https://teamworks.com/player-personnel-nfl)

**Subject**
Licensing inquiry for 2011–2012 NFL game-level player snap records

**Body**

Hello Teamworks Football Data team,

I’m building REDRAFT, a public NFL draft simulator with a historical 2018 evaluation. I’m looking for a one-time licensed extract from the former PFF enterprise NFL archive for all 512 regular-season games in 2011 and 2012, plus a small 2013 validation sample. I can use a cohort-only extract if it covers the supplied 2011 and 2012 combine quarterbacks in all games and positions and includes supporting evidence for games in which they did not play.

Can you provide exact offensive, defensive and special-teams snap counts by player, team and game, with stable game and player IDs or a crosswalk, a coverage manifest, and active/did-not-play, inactive and missing-data semantics? CSV or Parquet is suitable. Please share the phase-counting rules, whether the archive records all three phases for 2011 and 2012, and whether a 2013 sample can be licensed for comparison with official gamebooks and the public nflverse series.

The intended use is private storage and reproducible analysis, fitting and testing a statistical model, publishing aggregate evaluation findings, and deploying the resulting model or calibrated tables in the public simulator. Source tables can stay private. Could you identify the rights holder for this archive and quote a one-time extract under written terms covering those specific uses, including attribution, retention and any restrictions on publishing derived results? Please also include any academic or small-project pricing or evaluation-sample terms that are available.

Thank you.

**Subject**
Routing request for historical NFL gamebook participation records and permissions

**Body**

Hello NFL Support,

I would use such figures privately for data validation and model training, and publish aggregate evaluation findings and the resulting model or calibrated tables in the public simulator. The gamebook PDFs and raw tables can stay private. Can you identify the appropriate rights holder and tell me whether it offers a complete archive, defines the meaning of blank phases and nonparticipant statuses, and can authorize those specific uses? If another organization licenses this historical archive on the league’s behalf, please point me to the correct team.

Thank you.

## Sports Reference — 2012 fallback with known custom-download minimum

[Sports Reference feedback form](https://www.sports-reference.com/feedback/)

**Subject**
2012 Pro Football Reference snap data availability and licensing inquiry

**Body**

Hello Sports Reference,

I’m building REDRAFT, a public NFL draft simulator. I’m seeking written permission for a one-time 2012 extract of Pro Football Reference player-by-game snap counts for the 256 regular-season games, with stable identifiers, exact counts for all three phases, source definitions and evidence distinguishing missing rows from confirmed nonparticipation. A smaller extract for supplied quarterback identities is also useful if it has the same game coverage. I understand your published custom download policy specifies a $5,000 minimum and am inquiring about availability and terms.

I found a 2012 CSV circulated by nflscraPy that appears to derive from your game-level statistics. Could you clarify whether the project could use that release with your written permission, or whether your own export and any upstream approval would be required? The proposed use is private storage and validation, model training, aggregate evaluation reporting and public deployment of the resulting model or calibrated tables. Source rows can stay private. Please let me know whether these uses can be authorized, whether attribution or other conditions would apply, and whether a small licensed or evaluation sample is available.

Thank you.
