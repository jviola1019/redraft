# Cleveland pre-draft personnel, 2025

The team snapshot uses a calendar cutoff of April 23, 2025, the day before the draft began. The scope is selected Cleveland quarterback, defensive-front, and coaching facts. All notes use short factual paraphrases of public team announcements, and each entry links to its evidence. The panel does not infer a full April roster, a starting depth chart, return dates, scheme deployment rates, or positional vacancy. These observations do not enter CPU strategy, projection, or simulation.
