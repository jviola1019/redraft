# College disclosure and blind scouting

Pilot question: Does seeing the school increase correct player identification or change which player a participant would scout? The two randomized forms show the same eight pairs of 2018 prospects. Each pair shares a production blind profile and each volunteer sees a pair once. Four pairs include colleges on Form A and the complementary four on Form B. No names, IDs or answer keys appear in participant pages. This controlled comparison represents a tie on the displayed football information; ordinary boards also contain players from different profiles.

Before recruitment assign volunteers randomly to A or B in shuffled pairs within self-reported experience groups (follows the NFL draft closely or does not). Use one form per volunteer, with no outside lookups. Participant pages randomize trial order locally, collect no login or contact details, and download responses to the volunteer's device. Make participation optional. The facilitator collects only files voluntarily returned. No recruitment, transmission or enrollment is automated.

Primary endpoint: exact normalized full-name identifications divided by player prompts, counting blank guesses as abstentions. Secondary endpoints: abstention frequency and the share choosing the first printed prospect, by pair and condition. Player A has the same identity across both forms. An observed between-group choice difference is descriptive; it cannot reveal how one individual would have chosen under both conditions. Report each pair, sample sizes by form and experience and missing cells. Standardize recognition differences equally across pairs with data from both arms. If either form is absent withhold the contrast. The study is exploratory; eight deliberately ambiguous pairs are a restricted sample. Never pool automated validation fixtures with volunteer responses.

Run `node research/recognition_trial/prepare.mjs KIT_DIRECTORY`. Give each participant only the assigned HTML file. Keep key.json with the facilitator. Run `python research/recognition_trial/analyze.py KIT_DIRECTORY/key.json response1.json response2.json ...`. Empty input reports zero collected participants. Review ambiguous spellings separately without changing the primary exact-match result.
