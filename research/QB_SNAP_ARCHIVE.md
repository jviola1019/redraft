# Historical snap records for the strict 2018 quarterback target

Checked 2026-11-20. Pro Football Reference (PFR) publishes 2012 snap-count pages for all 32 franchises and claims full regular-season coverage on each. Official NFL gamebooks also contain exact player counts in sampled 2011 and 2012 games. The empty nflverse 2012 release is a distribution gap. This audit verified page availability, checked specific counts, and identified permission and coverage gaps. It yielded zero additional verified five-season donor vectors.

The frozen target sums regular-season offense and defense snaps over five seasons and separately counts games with any recorded snap, including special teams. The 2012 cohort needs seasons 2012–2016, so only its rookie year falls outside verified nflverse snap coverage. The 2011 cohort needs seasons 2011–2015, including two missing years. Using the local research inputs, the 2012 combine cohort has 19 QB rows and 14 pre-existing PFR identifiers. The 2011 cohort has 18 QB rows and 16 identifiers. The remaining names require identity and participation checks. The schedule contains 256 completed regular-season games per season in 2011 and 2012.
